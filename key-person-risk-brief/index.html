When You Need It

A key person is leaving

Leadership needs to understand open commitments, fragile handoffs, project context, and risk areas quickly.

Years of records, no clear thread

Email, documents, LLM histories, meeting notes, exports, and attachments hold the story, but no one has time to read everything.

The team needs evidence, not guesses

Executives need a concise brief, but they also need to trace important claims back to source records.

How The Engagement Works

Step 1

Scope and sign

We review the departure event together, identify data sources, timeline, sensitive-material rules, SOW, and data authority.

Step 2

Secure intake

The client provides a controlled encrypted data package. We verify hashes, scan, and process inside an encrypted engagement workbench.

Step 3

Analyze and verify

We normalize, index, investigate, draft, verify citations, review coverage, and prepare the secure workspace.

Step 4

Deliver and close

Your team receives the brief, evidence trail, workspace access, walkthrough, and deletion confirmation at closeout.

Data Security

We take your confidential data seriously. Client records are handled through a secure, single-client workflow: written data authority, controlled intake, an encrypted engagement workbench, restricted access, and deletion at closeout.

Commercial LLM assistance is part of the workflow. Current processing uses Amazon Bedrock with account data retention set to none. Client records are not used to train base models; the SOW and data-handling terms disclose provider use, retention limits, and platform safety obligations before any client records are processed.

Boundaries

Key-Person Risk Brief is

  • a white-glove continuity-risk engagement;
  • source-cited and human-reviewed;
  • focused on records the client is authorized to provide;
  • built for urgent executive decision support.

Key-Person Risk Brief is not

  • legal advice;
  • forensics or chain-of-custody evidence handling;
  • eDiscovery;
  • a HIPAA, privilege, HR, or compliance determination;
  • a promise of complete detection or no blind spots.
